Opening splash music: put songs (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, flac) here. One is picked at random to play behind the sparkler opening,
and it keeps playing on the title screen until you go into the menus.
Browsers only allow sound after a click or key press, so on a first visit with no click yet the opening may be silent.
Netlify finds the songs by itself on every deploy. On other hosts (GitHub Pages...) run  node tools/make-playlist.js  before uploading.
