Game switches

Game modes

Ticked: players see it and can play it. Unticked: it's hidden everywhere (the lobby, playlists, online rooms, matchmaking). A group's box turns all of its games on or off at once. Players' games pick this up within a minute (and at every match).

Player settings

Starting value: what a setting starts at (and what "Back to defaults" in the game puts it back to). Players who still had the old starting value move to the new one; anyone who had picked their own keeps it. Leave it blank for the game's own. Lock: the setting is the same for everyone (the starting value, or the game's own if blank) and shows 🔒 in the game's menus. Players' games pick this up within a minute.

Careful: this edits the stored JSON directly. Player records are under u/<name>; use the Players tab for everyday changes.
