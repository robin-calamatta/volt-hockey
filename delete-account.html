Delete your Volt Hockey account

Deleting your account permanently removes, straight away:

  • your login (username and password)
  • your email address
  • your saved settings and game progress
  • your place on the leaderboard
  • your friends list (you're removed from your friends' lists too) and your private messages

Reports made about an account may be kept for safety and legal reasons. Copies in backups are removed as the backups expire. Anything saved in your own browser or app stays until you clear it.

You can also do this in the game: open Friends, then Delete account. If you can't sign in (for example you forgot your password), use "Forgot password" in the game first, or contact us through the details in the privacy notice.
